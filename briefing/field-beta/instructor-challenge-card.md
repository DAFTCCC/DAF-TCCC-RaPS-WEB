# Instructor challenge: break the workflow, improve RaPS

Interrupt tasks, make deliberate mistakes, and tell us what slows you down or works well.

**40 minutes:** choose four assigned missions, including mission 8. Cover all eight across the cohort/week. Mark unfinished work NOT RUN. Finalization/closure use a planned SME case with time for existing requirements.

**Start:** confirm `3.4.12-web.9` and connected sign-in on both devices. Use individual accounts, TEST, your assigned installation and fictitious learners. Normal permissions apply. Keep one active claim per evaluator; reuse it for missions 2–4.

**Each mission:** **□ PASS □ ISSUE □ BLOCKED □ NOT RUN; □ Independent □ Help needed.** Expected denial/recovery can be a PASS.

## 1. Get in—and try the wrong door

**Try:** Sign in with your username/temporary password, choose a new password, then sign in again. Confirm role/base. An installation-scoped evaluator tries the facilitator's unassigned class at another installation.

**Expected:** Setup blocks training access until completed. Authorized scope opens; the unassigned class is absent or blocked. MAJCOM oversight alone grants no grading takeover.

**Report:** Login friction, unclear scope, surprising access or denial. Never record a password or token.

## 2. Tap fast, make a mistake, recover

**Try:** Claim the approved synthetic case online. In OBSERVE, record scripted PASS/FAIL, tap rapidly, deliberately mistap once, then Undo. Mark moment on an ungraded item. In REVIEW, save partial evidence, assistance, a contributor hypothesis, and coaching notes.

**Expected:** FAIL records immediately; Mark moment changes no grade/denominator. Undo corrects the latest eligible observation with an audit event. Coaching does not change official results.

**Report:** Missed cues, duplicate/unintended grades, confusing correction, or time looking away from the learner.

## 3. Lose the network after claiming

**Try:** On that claimed attempt, disconnect without closing RaPS. Add two identifiable observations and a note. Try finalizing/voiding; record exact messages. Reconnect and sync; verify entries from the second authorized device. Only after confirmation, sign out/in.

**Expected:** Your existing claim records locally while cached authorization remains valid. New starts/finalize/void require the server. Local-save and cloud status differ; reconnect/login preserves entries and attribution.

**Report:** Missing/duplicate entries, status wording, reconnect delay, or false success. A one-active-claim denial alone does not prove offline enforcement.

## 4. Interrupt a running timer

**Try:** Read the displayed standard. Start a timer, background/return, then actually reload while active. Follow recovery. Separately try PASS/NT/Undo after a scripted timing failure.

**Expected:** Actual reload requires **VOID AND RESTART**; resume/stop is blocked. Give a VOID reason, start a new instance and repeat the approved performance. PASS/Undo cannot bypass a timing failure; a started linked timer blocks NT. Recovery is expected.

**Report:** Recovery clarity, stopwatch comparison, and the next action.

## 5. Make the screen work harder

**Try:** Use long fictitious learner/class names and multiline notes. Rotate phone/tablet; open the keyboard, enlarge text, use gloves, and try safe sunlight/noisy conditions.

**Expected:** You can identify the learner, read criteria/timers, reach controls, and retain saved text.

**Report:** Clipping, hidden controls, accidental taps, keyboard obstruction, and helpful display choices.

## 6. Compete for the same event

**Try:** A claims; separately signed-in B opens the same assigned event and tries to claim/edit. If assigned, A also creates divergent edits across two devices using the facilitator's disposable conflict case.

**Expected:** B cannot silently take over or overwrite A. Any actual conflict requires inspection; compare LOCAL/SERVER before choosing. A denied claim need not show a conflict badge.

**Report:** Who changed what, device order, messages, retained branches, and resolution effort.

## 7. Challenge final results and totals

**Try:** Use the planned SME-verified case; satisfy existing requirements before server finalization. Try editing afterward. Authorized PM/MAJCOM/Enterprise observers reconcile the same case and installation totals.

**Expected:** Finalized fields are read-only; All MAJCOMs includes TEST. Reporting follows normal installation scope. PM closure retains history.

**Report:** Editable final fields, unexplained totals, or unclear provisional/final labels. Owner-only local-copy/trash/archive demonstrations follow the facilitator guide.

## 8. Your workflow, your challenge—5 minutes

**Try:** Follow your usual workflow using ordinary interface actions. Find one friction point; identify one thing worth keeping.

**Expected:** Useful feedback can be positive, negative, or a missing capability.

**Report:** “I tried… expected… observed… impact… suggested improvement…” Include exact steps, synthetic case ID, role, device/browser, version, and a sanitized screenshot if useful.

**Stop and notify the facilitator:** unauthorized/live detail, wrong-record changes, lost/overwritten grading, editable finalized results, or false save/finalization success. Keep the affected device state; never clear unsynced browser data. Do not share accounts, expose credentials, probe infrastructure, or alter clinical standards.
